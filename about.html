About

DANIELDE JONG

Daniel de Jong is a sound designer, field recordist and composer from the north-west of Germany. He studied music design in Trossingen and works between three practices that keep feeding each other: sound for film, long recordings of quiet places, and experimental music released as Aron Stadler.

What connects them is listening. A field recording describes a place and the life in it at one particular hour; a film needs that same attention to space; and in the music the question turns inward, to the point where noise becomes tone and an intention gives way to what is simply there.

Practice
Sound design, mixing and location sound for film · field recording · composition
Recording
